# Cancellation requires the company login

Bilag 1 ("Cancellation") asks for a secure link in the booking confirmation and the reminder that leads to a confirmation page showing the current fee. #5 built that as a public page: an HMAC-signed link that cancelled through the service-role client without a session. Anyone the mail was forwarded to could cancel. The link now goes through the company login instead: "Se eller afbook bookingen" in Mail 4, 5 and 6 is `/login?next=/bookings?booking=<id>`, so a visitor who is logged out logs in first, one who is logged in passes straight on, and Bookinger opens that booking's sheet, where "Aflys booking" shows Platform message 1 with the live fee and cancels under the company's session and RLS. Login only follows a `next` that is a path on this site. Nothing is lost for bookers: only someone logged in to the company account can make a booking (ADR-0004), and admin bookings are always for a company that has an account (ADR-0023). The public cancel page, the signing secret `BOOKING_CANCEL_SECRET` and the service-role path for cancelling are gone. Decided 2026-10-07 in #88.
